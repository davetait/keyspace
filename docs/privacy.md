# Keyspace Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Keyspace is a home screen app made by Key Space Apps. It does not collect, sell, or share your data.

## No internet access

Keyspace has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are no ads, analytics, or accounts.

## What stays on your phone

To work, Keyspace saves a few things on your phone only:

- Your layout: which apps and widgets are where.
- Your settings.
- Which apps you open and how often, so search shows your favorites first. You can erase this in Settings under "Clear search history".

Uninstalling Keyspace deletes all of it. If you turn on Android backup, Android may include these settings in your backup, under Google's privacy policy.

## Microphone

The microphone key lets you search by voice. Keyspace only listens after you tap it, and stops when you finish speaking or tap it again. Your speech goes to your phone's speech recognition service (for example, Google's), which turns it into text. That service may process audio under its own privacy policy. Keyspace never records or saves audio.

## Notification dots

If you turn on notification dots and allow notification access, Keyspace checks which apps have notifications so it can show a dot on their icons. It never reads, saves, or shares what your notifications say.

## Your app list

Keyspace reads the list of apps installed on your phone to show and search them. This list never leaves your phone.

## Children

Keyspace collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
